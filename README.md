# Rock Road Film Shelf

A browsable catalogue of our film, TV and documentary collection: search, mood filters, IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes scores, "pick for me", and seen/favourite tracking (stored in your browser).

Single static page (`index.html`), no build step. Poster art is loaded from Wikipedia/Wikimedia and belongs to the respective studios and distributors.

Install on iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs full screen and works fully offline (the service worker saves the page, fonts and every poster on first launch).
